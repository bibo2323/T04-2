## Data Story

Our audience/consumer is Australian who are shopping for a new TV. They want an energy-efficient, good-value TV without needing to understand technical details. They prefer simple visuals and trusted brand names over raw data.

This section presents insights from an Australian Government dataset on TV energy use, helping these buyers make smarter, more informed choices when selecting a new television.

## About the Data

Data source:
The dataset used is from https://data.gov.au/ Australia’s public data.

Data processing:
The raw dataset was cleaned and processed using KNIME. This included grouping data by category (e.g., screen technology, brand, screen size), removing irrelevant columns, and aggregating values (count, mean) to answer each research question.

Privacy:
The dataset does not contain any personal or identifiable information — it only includes product-level data such as brand, model, screen size, and power consumption.

Accuracy and limitations:
The dataset only reflects TVs registered under the Australian energy rating scheme at the time of collection and may not include every model currently on the market. Some categories (e.g., OLED) had fewer entries, which may affect how representative the averages are.

Ethics:
The data was used solely for educational purposes to explore and communicate insights about TV energy consumption. No manipulation of the data was done beyond standard cleaning and aggregation to ensure findings remain accurate and unbiased.
